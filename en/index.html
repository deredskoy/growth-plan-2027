From clicks to accounts

Search clicks are getting scarcer and AI now answers the simple queries, so chasing another 20–30 Google clicks makes no sense. I propose pointing the infrastructure built for SEO at selling Plugin, Prime and the agency package, in three pilots for the first quarter of 2027.

The market supports the thesis

What the open sources show, and what they do not show yet.

The search click is getting scarcer

In January–April 2026, 68% of Google searches in the US ended without a click (SparkToro). When an AI answer sits above the results, the click-through rate of the first position is 58% lower (Ahrefs).

For Stripo: pages written for clicks are not growth.

AI drafts the email but does not produce it

The agents from Klaviyo, Customer.io and Iterable already build campaigns and emails inside the ESP. Yet only 1.1% of email professionals start a new email from scratch in AI (Really Good Emails), and 88% of enterprise marketing leaders say AI output needs editing (Demand Gen Report).

For Stripo: the draft is getting cheaper, but reliable HTML, brand modules and export to any ESP are not. That is what Plugin and Prime sell.

Money is moving to embeddable editors

Beefree SDK is growing 18% while its end-user app grows 7% (Growens). Unlayer closed its Studio app on 30 June 2026 (Unlayer), and Beefree raised its prices in May (Beefree pricing).

For Stripo: the window for Plugin is open.

What is not visible yet

Stripo’s own trend cannot be measured from public data: the Semrush and Similarweb traffic estimates for stripo.email differ by almost a factor of two.

First step of the plan: Google Search Console data for 24 months, by site section. It will show where the “peak” is real.

1 January 2027

HubSpot starts rating marketplace partners against the full requirements: the Rising tier needs at least 5 reviews in 12 months with an average of 3.5★ or higher. Stripo is now at 3.6★ from 18 ratings (listing, program rules). The first window for a downgrade is the first quarter of 2027. Pilot 1 handles this.

The value is in the engines, not in the sites

The satellite sites brought no meaningful traffic. But the systems that produced them can do what sales needs.

Site factory and admin

Multi-tenant engine, 54 design packs, roles, a “draft, owner approval, publish” flow and an audit log. The admin has 4 active users: 3,106 audit-log events from May to October.

For B2B: a page for one customer, with their emails in their brand and a Plugin sandbox.

domain-scout enrichment pipeline

Candidates, paid enrichment within a budget (Ahrefs: 9 units per domain instead of 100), rules, a spreadsheet and a Telegram bot. 788 automated tests.

For B2B: a weekly “who to call” list for sales.

HTML page publisher

Versions in R2, automatic domains and certificates, checks before publishing.

For B2B: a personal page in minutes.

LLM pipeline

Draft, links from web search, quality check.

For B2B: honest Plugin comparisons and pre-demo briefs, always edited by a human.

Built solo in eight months, about 300 commits in the main project. Hosting costs about $12 a month (Cloudflare and Render, May 2026 data).

Three pilots for the first quarter of 2027

About 85 of the quarter’s 120 hours. Each pilot has a KPI and a stop point at 90 days: if the goal is missed, the hours move to the next project.

Pilot 1, 30 h30–40 to launch, then about 2 h a month

Partner tier in HubSpot and Klaviyo

Stripo’s visibility in the HubSpot marketplace (1M+ visits a month) survives the first tier review under the new rules. For Klaviyo Preferred, which needs more than $10M of associated ARR, an assessment and an application package are prepared.

The deadline is firm: from 1 January 2027 the Rising tier needs at least 5 reviews in 12 months with an average of 3.5★ or higher. Stripo has 3.6★ from 18 ratings; the latest reviews are 5★ in August and 2★ in May 2026.

Success at 90 days
At least 5 new reviews in the quarter with an average of 4.0★ or higher. Listing leads reach the CRM within 24 hours. A go/no-go decision on Klaviyo Preferred is made from the associated-ARR estimate.
First step in 30 days
Get access to the HubSpot and Klaviyo portals, export 12 months of reviews, calculate the gap to the threshold and, together with support, send the first wave of requests to active users of the integration.

Basis: the HubSpot marketplace gets 1M+ visits a month (program rules); partners and their customers brought in about 49% of HubSpot’s 2025 revenue (10-K); the Klaviyo Preferred threshold (program guide).

Pilot 2, 40 hanother 25 h once access to usage data is granted

Account intelligence: who is ready to buy

Every week sales gets a list of 20 accounts, each with an explanation of its score. Three sources: corporate domains where Stripo is already in use (once the data is available); SaaS products with embedded Beefree or Unlayer editors; certified agencies from the Klaviyo, HubSpot, Braze and Brevo directories.

A brief on the company before every demo. The foundation is the domain-scout engine, reused whole.

Success at 90 days
At least 150 qualified accounts in 60 days, at least 30% of them accepted by sales, at least 8 meetings. Pipeline is recorded in the CRM.
First step in 30 days
Build a top 50 from public data and show it to sales together with the scoring logic. In parallel, request an aggregated export by domain and clear it with legal.

Basis: 70% of Figma’s new Organization and Enterprise customers already had a paying user, and 2.5% of customers paying $10K or more produce 64% of ARR (SaaStr on the S-1); Canva shows admins a report on users with corporate email domains (Canva); 2,491 public repositories depend on react-email-editor (GitHub).

Pilot 3, 15 hmanual pilot; another 45 h for automation in the second quarter

Personal pages: “your emails are already in Stripo”

For an account from pilot 2, a private page is built: 3–5 emails in the company’s brand, created through the official Stripo MCP, with rendering and accessibility checks and a button to book a meeting.

For Plugin, a sandbox: the Stripo editor in the colors of the customer’s product. For agencies, a kit for presenting to their client.

Success at 90 days
20–30 pages in 60 days. At least twice as many positive replies as in a control group without a page. At least 5 meetings. Building one page takes no more than 30 minutes.
First step in 30 days
Build 5 pages by hand for accounts chosen by sales and review them together, before any automation.

Basis (vendor case studies): a personal page tripled the number of booked meetings in LeadCat’s A/B test (GenPage); microsites “influenced $640K of ARR” at Simpro (Mutiny). No published results were found for the “your emails are already in our editor” approach from email-builder vendors.

What it pays back
