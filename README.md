# growth-plan-2027

Static landing page: Ukrainian at `/`, English at `/en/`. There is no build step. Edit `index.html`, `en/index.html` and `assets/`, push to `main`, and GitHub Pages republishes.

The pages are marked `noindex`. Font: Onest (SIL OFL 1.1), licence in `assets/fonts/OFL-Onest.txt`.

The neutral, grayscale notes use native HTML details for ideas; they work without JavaScript. Keep the UA/EN content aligned.

Five substantial AI/software projects describe recurring workflows, existing components, new capabilities, delivery stages and measures of value. Hour estimates and the separate context block are omitted.

Expanded projects use a wide layout, process diagrams and thin timeline charts inspired by AI 2027. Four charts model scenarios using stated inputs; the fifth shows historical Ahrefs estimates only. The full research exports stay outside this public repository.

Chart data checked on 12 November 2026:
- Planning: 3,362 US keywords for stripo.email from Ahrefs; hypothetical one-check-per-keyword workflow.
- Links: 7 of 47 templates receive links from other template pages in the audited newsletter gallery. This is not total internal reachability or Google indexing.
- Website screening: 5,198 domains link to Beefree according to Ahrefs; not qualified opportunities or a confirmed link gap.
- Catalog search: six of eight selected phrases return no match under the existing whole-query substring filter applied to exact public gallery inputs. This is a diagnostic sample, not visitor logs or a relevance benchmark.
- Reporting: Ahrefs US organic-traffic estimates at the first of each month, June–November 2026. No traffic uplift forecast is attached to the analytics system.

Scenario formulas (round display only):
- Automation share ramps linearly to the selected target over the selected rollout months.
- Workload: N × (1 − automation × (1 − review)). One item and one manual review each count as one check; this does not estimate time or wages.
- Link coverage: 7 + (47 − 7) × share of gaps closed.
- Empty searches: 6 × (1 − share resolved). Relevant templates must actually exist.

Ahrefs numbers are third-party estimates; their methodology and scope are linked in each chart. Raw responses, credentials, quota details and account identifiers must not be added to the site.
